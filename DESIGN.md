## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, clinical yet deeply empathetic presence for modern companion animal medicine. It blends the clinical competence of human-grade healthcare with the comfort and approachability necessary to de-escalate anxiety for pet parents in distress.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Warm Organic Modernism**—a synthesis of high-contrast medical clarity and tactile organic softness. It combines generous negative space, soft ambient illumination, and gentle organic curves to communicate safety, modern diagnostics, and unconditional care.

## Colors

The palette establishes clinical authority and organic vitality through layered forest tones, cushioned by soft mint washes and warm slate typography:

- **Primary (`#059669`)**: Forest Green. Anchor for core brand interactions, primary actions, and confirmed health indicators.
- **Secondary (`#064E3B`)**: Deep Evergreen. Reserved for structural typography, high-priority navigation bars, footers, and authoritative medical disclosures.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Vivid Emerald. Used selectively for progress indicators, online booking CTAs, active status dots, and focus highlights.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1E293B`)**: Deep Slate. Provides high-contrast, fatigue-free readability across all primary content.
- **Neutral Muted (`#64748B`)**: Cool Slate. Used for metadata, caption text, form labels, and subtle outlines.
- **Backgrounds & Surfaces**: Clean Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) forms the foundational canvas, supported by Mint Whisper (`#ECFDF5`) for elevated card surfaces, section breaks, and informational tinting, and Mint Foam (`#D1FAE5`) for hover states, pill backgrounds, and badge containers.
- **Emergency Accent (`#DC2626`)**: Vivid Crimson. Dedicated exclusively to 24/7 urgent care triage, crisis banners, and immediate triage actions.

## Typography

The design system relies entirely on Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver an approachable, human, yet meticulously engineered typographic hierarchy. 

- **Headlines**: Employ tight letter-spacing and substantial weights (700–800) to project confidence, authority, and modern medical standards.
- **Body Content**: Employs normal weights (400) with generous line heights (1.6x) to preserve readability during emotionally sensitive situations, such as post-operative guidance or triage advice.
- **Labels & Badges**: Feature uppercase tracking or punchy semi-bold weights for rapid legibility in diagnostic badges, open-hour indicators, and interactive micro-data.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column responsive fluid grid enforces visual order while preserving airy layouts:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 2rem gutters, with a maximum centered container width of 1280px. Outer margins scale to 4rem.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 1.5rem gutters, and 2rem outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 1rem gutters, and 1rem outer canvas margins.

Vertical rhythm is anchored on an 8px base grid. Section blocks maintain generous vertical spacing (4rem to 6rem) to prevent cognitive overload.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **ambient, emerald-tinted drop shadows** paired with soft, low-contrast structural borders:

- **Surface 0 (Base)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted mint canvas (`#ECFDF5`).
- **Surface 1 (Card & Container)**: Pure white background overlaying `#ECFDF5`, bound by an ultra-thin 1px border (`#D1FAE5` or `#E2E8F0`). Shadow: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.04), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.02)`.
- **Surface 2 (Interactive Hover / Popovers)**: Elevated cards on pointer hover and interactive tooltips. Shadow: `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.03)`.
- **Surface 3 (Modals / Emergency Flyouts)**: Prominent triage dialogs and overlays. Shadow: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes friendliness, soft containment, and safety:

- **Cards & Functional Modules**: Fixed at `rounded-2xl` (1rem to 1.5rem) to soften content perimeters and avoid clinical harshness.
- **Badges, Buttons & Filters**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) to invite touch and reinforce the friendly, approachable brand identity.
- **Form Controls & Inputs**: Balanced at `rounded-xl` (0.75rem) for clean alignment with adjacent typography and iconography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Forest Green (`#059669`) fill, white text, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `px-6 py-3.5`. Hover shifts to `#047857` with a subtle elevation transition (`translate-y-[-1px]`).
- **Secondary**: Soft Mint (`#D1FAE5`) fill with Deep Evergreen (`#064E3B`) text. Hover darkens to `#A7F3D0`.
- **Urgent / Emergency**: Vivid Crimson (`#DC2626`) fill, white text, with an integrated pulse animation indicator for after-hours triage.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges**: `rounded-full`, `px-3.5 py-1`, text style `label-sm`.
- **Status Mint**: Background `#D1FAE5`, text `#064E3B`, featuring an active emerald dot.
- **Emergency / Urgent Care**: Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#991B1B`.

### Cards
- **Content & Service Cards**: Base `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, rounded `rounded-2xl`, internal padding `p-6` to `p-8`. Includes smooth hover state: subtle border color shift to `#A7F3D0` and Surface 2 elevation.
- **Pricing Cards**: Feature an inner Mint Whisper (`#ECFDF5`) header container, clear itemized list rows with custom forest-green checkmarks, and an anchored pill CTA. Highlighted "Wellness Plans" feature an outer 2px stroke in `#059669` and a floating "Most Popular" pill badge.
- **Interactive Map Cards**: Integrated preview container featuring rounded-2xl map iframe framing, overlaid with a floating Surface-1 panel showing live clinic hours, real-time "Open Now" badges, and direct navigation links.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, `rounded-xl`, `px-4 py-3`, text style `body-md`. Focus state: 2px ring in `#10B981` with zero layout shift.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Emerald `#059669` active states with soft-curved borders (`rounded-md` for checks, `rounded-full` for radios).

### Emergency Callout Banner
- Full-width container in Soft Rose (`#FEF2F2`) with an Emerald/Crimson accent bar, clear phone dispatch icons, and high-contrast click-to-call buttons for instant crisis triage.
